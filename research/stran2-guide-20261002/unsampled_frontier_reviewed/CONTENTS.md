# Included versus referenced evidence

## Included verbatim

- Complete newly generated four-case run (130 files), including baseline candidates,
  frozen returned frontiers, root counters, candidate-local full free models,
  all new CNFs, ordered queries, raw status receipts and source/model bindings
- Complete newly generated mem attribution run (156 files), including104 freshly
  rechecked Build triples and312 Direct triples, all candidate sets,28 actual
  product AIGs/selections, removal/dominance results and17 fresh DSEC logs
- The audit reuses previously recorded full Build witnesses and independently
  replays them before its fresh status-only triple queries. It does not claim to
  extract a new SAT model for each of those fresh audit queries. Direct records
  carry sampled witnesses only where present; no unwitnessed free-SAT result is
  used to claim a new genuine Build
- All four original first-SEQ AIG inputs and full historical frozen_inputs.json
  snapshots: exact domains, roots/order/pools/configuration and provenance hashes
- Exact diagnostic/product source, protocols, tests and source manifests;
  the seven original frozen runtime Python sources; whole-original-hash and
  grammar-Direct helper sources; solver prebuild/build manifests
- Independent gate/terminal reviews, historical authentication, compact metrics
  and the review scripts explicitly referenced by the final review wrappers
-18 portable primary input/product/log pairs, indexed in compact/portable_primary_index.json.
  Their log copies preserve exact bytes, including original command paths. Reused
  certificates can share the same log hash. They are not18 fresh DSEC calls

## Referenced by identity, not bundled

- ABC/solver executables, libabc.a, compiler binaries and system/header bytes.
  Tool/build manifests preserve their paths, hashes and build commands
- Older precursor raw runs under /tmp/nonlinear-anytime-pilot-v1 and
  /tmp/image-cegar-diagnostic-v1, including historical model receipts, old proof
  batches, complete root-search logs and ancillary jobs beyond the exported
  frozen snapshots. Full old A/B/C/random-arm run trees and earlier historical
  archives are not included
- Historical A/B/union52 product records and their old DSEC executions are
  authenticated in historical_authentication.json. The new mixed graph bytes
  equal historical B/union52, but that identity does not mean old logs were rerun

The raw trees and sources retain original experiment-bound absolute paths. Use
EXPORT_MANIFEST.json to locate exported copies. Relocating paths or building new
executables requires a new bound plan and new evidence, not rewriting historical
identities. Portable AIGs can be inspected or newly verified independently. This
bundle is not claimed to be self-contained reproduction of every precursor proof.

No solver/DSEC or algorithm experiment was run to create this export. The
single-design policy is stopped. Paired improvement+1, raw-new removal7 and
mixed-new removal3 remain distinct; mixed output matches the known historical+1.
